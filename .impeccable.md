## Design Context

### Users
Potential employers and creative clients exploring Diego's projects, capabilities, and approach before getting in touch.

### Brand Personality
Minimal, warm, approachable — the direction explicitly selected by Diego. Communicate care and confidence through clear, friendly language and considered details.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, spacious layouts with Instrument Sans, warm neutral accents, and project imagery providing the strongest color. Editorial hierarchy and gentle asymmetry should make the work easy to explore.

References supplied by Diego: https://www.ericsin.com/ (direct gallery, compact captions and filters), https://brandonyasin.com/cases/bestseller (warm tones, generous space and image composition), and https://gilhuybrecht.com/ (visual browsing and alternative gallery layouts). Combine these qualities while retaining Diego's own project assets and identity.

### Design Principles
- Let project imagery lead; keep the surrounding interface restrained.
- Use generous spacing and clear typography to establish hierarchy.
- Keep captions readable and interactions obvious on desktop and mobile.
- Distinguish available case studies from upcoming work.
- Respect reduced motion and maintain keyboard access.
